# Privacy Policy - ChatGPT Gradient Theme

**Last updated:** July 16, 2026

## Summary

ChatGPT Gradient Theme skins `chatgpt.com` with a dark color theme, a local WebGL gradient canvas, and Alt keyboard shortcuts. The extension does not collect, store, or send personal information, browsing history, or page content to the developer or any third party.

## Local storage

No `chrome.storage` or other persistence. Theme and gradient run only while the page is open.

## When you use the extension

- Content scripts inject CSS and JS on `https://chatgpt.com/*` at `document_idle`.
- A WebGL canvas draws the gradient in your browser.
- Alt+N, Alt+L, and Alt+I click ChatGPT UI controls already on the page.
- No analytics or server communication from the extension.

## Where data goes

Processing stays in your browser on chatgpt.com. No backend server. No cloud sync. Chat text stays in the page OpenAI already serves.

## Third-party services

No analytics, ads, or third-party SDKs in the extension package.

## Permissions

| Access | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `https://chatgpt.com/*` (content scripts) | Apply the theme, gradient, and shortcuts on ChatGPT |

No `activeTab`, `scripting`, `storage`, or host access beyond chatgpt.com.

## Your controls

- **Remove the skin:** Uninstall from `chrome://extensions`, or disable the extension.
- **Rebind shortcuts:** `chrome://extensions/shortcuts`.

## Children

ChatGPT Gradient Theme is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

This policy updates when practices change. The date at the top changes with it. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
